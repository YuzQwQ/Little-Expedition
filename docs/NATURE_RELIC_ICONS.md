# 自然派系遗物图标

使用内置 ImageGen 逐件绘制。透明 PNG 以 256 × 256 像素导出，并在 48 像素下检查轮廓。文件位于 `public/assets/relics/nature/`，文件名与游戏中的遗物 ID 一致。

每张图的提示词由固定前缀、表中的主体描述和固定画风要求依次组成。

前缀

> Little Expedition nature faction inventory icon.

固定画风要求

> One isolated centered complete object, square icon, generous clear padding, true transparent background. Slightly overhead 3/4 view, upper-left soft warm light, cool shadow within object, muted natural green, warm ochre and cream. Hand-painted storybook fantasy, soft watercolor and restrained painterly shading, subtle paper texture inside silhouette, clean dark brown hand-drawn outlines, bold shape legible at 48px. No backdrop, cast shadow, border, text, lettering, sparkles, extra objects, glossy 3D, neon, heavy black outlines or excessive ornament.
